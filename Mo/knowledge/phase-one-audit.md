# Phase One — Board Audit Plan (from Ayrica, 2026-10-26)

Mode: **AUDIT MODE.** Nothing changes on the boards until Ayrica approves it. All proposed changes are recorded first.

## Step 1 — Inventory all five boards
For each: name & purpose · groups & intended function · columns & status definitions · existing automations · recurring task templates · assigned employees · related boards · current SOPs.
**Deliverable:** Board Structure Inventory.

## Step 2 — Identify organization gaps
Review open tasks + relevant history for: missing owners · missing due dates · missing action summaries · outdated statuses · in-review tasks without specific questions · missing recurring periods · duplicates · unresolved inbound requests · cross-board mismatches · tasks that may be complete but lack documented closure.
**Deliverable:** Board Audit Findings Report.

## Step 3 — Categorize by priority
P1 Compliance & Risk · P2 Customer Impact · P3 Cash Flow · P4 Capacity & Improvement.
**Deliverable:** Prioritized Correction Queue.

## Step 4 — Standardize action summaries
For every active task: read description, updates, and source docs → current objective → actions done vs outstanding → concise summary → next responsible person → open question/approval → (after approval) pin summary or fill the action-summary field.
Distinguish confirmed facts from assumptions; flag unclear records rather than invent completion details.
**Deliverable:** Standardized Task Action Summaries.

## Step 5 — Reconcile related tasks across boards
Match on verified client identifiers, entity names, service periods, document references. Decide whether info on one board: resolves a task · requires an update elsewhere · creates a new task · changes a deadline · needs client follow-up · needs professional review. Record proposed changes for approval.
**Deliverable:** Cross-Board Reconciliation Report.

## Step 6 — Validate recurring services & deadlines (Monthly Client Board)
Compare the active month group vs last month's group vs the Template group. Recommend tasks to add to Template. Verify client-information fields are complete on each group/template. Flag gaps for human confirmation before creating historical tasks or treating work as missed.
**Deliverable:** Recurring Services Completeness Report.

## Step 7 — Review & approve corrections
Ayrica reviews findings and authorizes corrections. Once approved: update owners, summaries, statuses, dates, relationships. Log what changed, why, who authorized (`memory/approval-log.md`).
**Deliverable:** Phase One Board Cleanup and Approval Log.

## Acceptance criteria (Phase One isn't done until all true)
- [ ] All five boards have documented purposes and ownership
- [ ] Every active task has an accountable owner
- [ ] Every active task has a clear next action or documented reason for waiting
- [ ] Every active task has a current, visible action summary
- [ ] Sent for Review tasks identify a specific question, draft, or approval
- [ ] Recurring client tasks checked against the active service roster
- [ ] Missing reporting periods identified and resolved or assigned
- [ ] Stale/potentially inaccurate statuses reviewed
- [ ] Cross-board relationships and dependencies evaluated
- [ ] All high-priority compliance, customer, cash-flow exceptions have owner + resolution plan
- [ ] Team approved standard operating rules for ongoing board maintenance
- [ ] Audit log and recurring monitoring schedule established

## Operating directive
**Mission:** Maintain complete, accurate, organized, actionable monday.com boards across all FFG service offerings.
1. Every task: clear purpose, accountable owner, next action.
2. Every task: concise, current action summary visible at top.
3. Every status accurately represents progress.
4. Every new document or client communication evaluated against existing tasks.
5. Every recurring service obligation represented and monitored.
6. Every relevant cross-board dependency identified and coordinated.
7. Every unresolved issue assigned to the right team member before escalating to executive management.
8. Escalate to leadership only: executive authority, material risk decisions, unresolved exceptions.
9. AI recommendations and actions traceable to source records.
10. Never assume work is complete without sufficient supporting evidence.

**Desired outcome:** monday.com is a reliable representation of client commitments, team responsibilities, and operational progress, so the team can execute independently.
